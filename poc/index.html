Three kinds of things

ProjectA branch. It can hold anything, including other projects, as deep as you like. Its ring fills as the todos inside get done.
TodoSomething to tick off. Give it todos of its own to break it into steps.
NoteInfo to keep next to your work. No checkbox, doesn’t count toward progress.

Shortcuts

TabAdd inside the selected item (then P T or N)
EnterAdd the next item of the same kind
SpaceMark a todo done or not done
KChange what kind of item it is
F2 or ERename
DeleteDelete the item and everything inside it
Arrow keysMove between items
Alt + ↑ ↓Move the item up or down
Alt + → ←Tuck under the item above, or move out a level
CCollapse or expand
FFit the tree on screen
Ctrl/⌘ + ZUndo

While typing a new item, Enter saves it and starts another of the same kind. Escape stops.

Drag empty space to move around. Pinch, or hold Ctrl/⌘ and scroll, to zoom.

Your tree is stored on this device and works offline. Use the download button to keep a backup or move it to another device, and the upload button to restore it.
